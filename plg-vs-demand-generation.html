Product-Led Growth vs Demand Generation: Which Strategy Fits Your SaaS Business?

Two ways to grow a SaaS business — through the product experience or through marketing-led demand — and how to decide which fits yours.

  • PLG uses the product experience to attract, engage and convert users, while demand generation builds interest through marketing activities.
  • The right choice depends on the product, target audience, pricing model and sales process.

Product-Led Growth vs Demand Generation

SaaS companies need a consistent way to attract new users and turn them into paying customers. Some businesses allow users to experience the product before purchasing, while others focus on creating demand through content, advertising and sales conversations.

Both approaches can work, but they serve different purposes.

What Is Product-Led Growth?

Product-Led Growth, commonly called PLG, is a business strategy where the product itself plays a central role in attracting and converting customers.

Instead of depending entirely on sales conversations, a SaaS company gives potential customers an opportunity to explore the product. This may happen through a free trial, freemium plan, interactive demo or limited-access account.

How PLG Works

A typical product-led journey looks like this:

  1. A user discovers the product through search, recommendations or content.
  2. The user signs up for a free trial or free plan.
  3. The user explores the product and tries its main features.
  4. The user experiences value and becomes more engaged.
  5. The user upgrades to a paid plan when the product meets their needs.

Platforms such as OpenView PLG Resources, Pendo Product Analytics and Amplitude Product Analytics offer resources and tools related to product experience and user behaviour.

Benefits of Product-Led Growth

PLG can reduce the effort required for users to understand a product before making a purchase. It also allows companies to learn from actual product usage.

Key benefits include:

  • Users can experience the product directly.
  • Product engagement provides useful customer insights.
  • Free plans can increase product awareness.
  • Happy users may recommend the product to others.
  • Product usage data can help identify upgrade opportunities.

However, PLG is not suitable for every SaaS business. Products that require complex implementation, training or enterprise-level approvals may need more support before customers can adopt them.

What Is Demand Generation?

Demand Generation focuses on creating awareness, building interest and helping potential customers understand why they need a particular solution.

It covers more than collecting leads. The purpose is to educate the target audience, build trust and create demand for the product.

How Demand Generation Works

A demand generation strategy usually includes:

  • SEO and educational content
  • Paid search and social campaigns
  • Webinars and online events
  • Email marketing
  • Industry reports and case studies
  • Product demonstrations and consultations

Resources such as HubSpot Marketing Resources, Google Ads Learning and Content Marketing Institute provide guidance on different marketing activities.

Benefits of Demand Generation

Demand generation is useful when potential customers need more information before making a purchase.

It can help SaaS businesses:

  • Reach a clearly defined target audience.
  • Explain complex product features.
  • Build credibility through useful content.
  • Generate demo requests and enquiries.
  • Support longer B2B buying journeys.

Product-Led Growth vs Demand Generation: Key Differences

1. Customer Acquisition

PLG attracts users by allowing them to experience the product. Demand generation attracts potential buyers through marketing communication and educational resources.

For example, a simple project management tool may offer a free plan, while an enterprise software platform may rely on detailed product demonstrations and sales discussions.

2. Buying Process

PLG works well when users can understand the product without extensive assistance. Demand generation is useful when purchasing decisions involve multiple people, budgets and approval processes.

3. Marketing Investment

PLG requires investment in product usability, onboarding, customer experience and analytics. Demand generation requires investment in content, advertising, campaigns and lead nurturing.

Neither approach is automatically cheaper. Costs depend on the product and customer acquisition model.

4. Performance Measurement

PLG businesses often track activation rate, product adoption, trial-to-paid conversion and retention.

Demand generation teams may track website traffic, qualified leads, cost per lead, demo bookings and pipeline contribution.

Tools such as Google Analytics, Google Search Console and Looker Studio can help businesses understand digital performance.

Which Strategy Is Right for Your SaaS Business?

The decision depends on how customers discover, evaluate and purchase your product.

Choose Product-Led Growth When

  • Your product is easy to understand and use.
  • Users can experience value without extensive support.
  • You can offer a useful free trial or freemium version.
  • Individual users can start using the product independently.
  • Your product has a straightforward onboarding process.

Choose Demand Generation When

  • Your product solves complex business problems.
  • Customers need demonstrations or consultations.
  • Buying decisions involve multiple stakeholders.
  • Your sales cycle is longer.
  • Your audience needs education before considering a purchase.

Can SaaS Businesses Use Both?

Yes. Many SaaS businesses can benefit from combining the two approaches.

Demand generation can bring potential customers to the website, while a free trial or product demo allows them to experience the solution.

For example, a SaaS company can publish educational blogs, run targeted advertising and generate product interest. Visitors can then sign up for a free trial and explore the product.

This creates a connected journey instead of treating marketing and product experience as separate activities.

FAQs

1. Is Product-Led Growth better than Demand Generation?

Neither is universally better. PLG works well for products that users can adopt independently, while demand generation is useful for products that require education, demonstrations or sales support.

2. Can a SaaS company use both strategies?

Yes. A SaaS company can use demand generation to attract and educate potential customers, then use free trials or product demonstrations to encourage adoption.

3. What are the main PLG metrics?

Common PLG metrics include activation rate, product adoption, trial-to-paid conversion, customer retention and expansion revenue.

4. What are the main demand generation metrics?

Businesses commonly track qualified leads, cost per lead, website engagement, demo bookings, pipeline contribution and customer acquisition cost.
