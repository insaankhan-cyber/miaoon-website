Privacy Policy

Who we are

Miaoon is a digital marketing, branding and technology agency operating in India. This policy applies to www.miaoon.in, Miaoon AI CRM, our business enquiry channels and our Facebook and Instagram lead forms. Where we determine how information is used, Miaoon is responsible for that processing. Where we process information on behalf of a client, the client's own privacy notice and our instructions from that client may also apply.

Information we collect

  • Contact information: name, email address, telephone number and company details you provide.
  • Business enquiries: services of interest, requirements, messages, meeting details and other information you choose to submit.
  • Advertising information: lead submission identifiers, Page, form, campaign and advertisement identifiers, platform and submission time, where available.
  • Technical information: IP address, device and browser information, website usage and referral information where collected by our website or service providers.
  • Business contact channels: publicly available company names, websites, generic business email addresses and business phone numbers used for business research.
  • Communications: correspondence and, where separately agreed, call transcripts and AI-generated assessments.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, financial account details or passwords through general enquiry forms.

How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries, provide requested information, proposals and consultations.
  • Manage leads, contacts, client relationships and service delivery.
  • Understand campaign performance, improve our website and measure marketing effectiveness.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Send relevant marketing communications only where permitted by law and consistent with your choices; obtain separate consent where required.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, processing may be based on your consent, steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legal obligations or legitimate business interests where legally permitted.

Facebook and Instagram lead forms

When you submit a Meta Instant Form for Miaoon, Meta may provide us with the contact details and answers you submit, along with available form and campaign identifiers. Our integration is designed to transfer this information into Miaoon AI CRM to manage and respond to your enquiry.

We use lead form data for the purpose disclosed in the form and any additional purposes for which we have a valid legal basis and necessary permissions. We do not sell Meta lead form data. We may share it with service providers acting on our behalf as necessary to fulfil the disclosed purpose. Meta processes information under its own Privacy Policy.

You may request that we stop contacting you or delete your information by emailing hello@miaoon.in.

CRM and AI-assisted processing

Miaoon AI CRM is used to organize business contacts, manage enquiries, track follow-ups and support sales workflows. Our AI tools may assist with conversation summaries, lead qualification and recommended next actions. AI output can be inaccurate and should be reviewed where appropriate.

AI-assisted telephone calls are subject to separate documented calling permission. Where our pilot requests consent to retain a call transcript or assessment, we seek that consent before saving it. Audio recording is not enabled by default in the current pilot. You may decline or withdraw consent and ask for human review of an AI-generated assessment.

Who may receive information

We may use service providers to support our operations, including:

  • Meta: advertising, lead forms and related API services.
  • Supabase: CRM database and authentication.
  • Render: website and backend hosting.
  • Twilio and Groq: telephone and AI processing, when relevant to an authorized interaction.
  • Other authorized hosting, analytics, communications or professional service providers as needed for the relevant service.

We may disclose information where legally required or to protect rights, safety and security. For client campaigns, information may be provided to the relevant client as disclosed in the campaign notice and permitted by applicable law. We do not authorize providers to use Meta lead data for unrelated purposes on our behalf.

Cookies and analytics

Our website or integrated services may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics, security and advertising measurement. You can manage cookies in your browser and use available platform or website privacy controls. Where required, non-essential tracking should operate only after the appropriate consent is obtained.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, manage business records and meet legal obligations. Retention depends on the purpose, applicable obligations and whether a business relationship remains active. We may retain limited suppression information to respect opt-out requests.

We use access controls and authenticated systems to help protect CRM information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. We will address suspected security incidents and provide notifications where required by applicable law.

Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing, withdrawal of consent, or a copy of your data. You may also request information about its use and raise a privacy grievance.

To exercise these choices, email hello@miaoon.in with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity using proportionate means. We aim to respond promptly and within applicable legal time limits. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing before withdrawal. You may opt out of promotional communications using the available unsubscribe mechanism or by contacting us.

Where applicable, you may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority after following any required grievance process.

International processing

Our services and technology providers may process information in India and other countries. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards and comply with applicable restrictions on international transfers. Local data protection rights may differ depending on your jurisdiction.

Children's privacy

Our business services and lead generation campaigns are intended for adults and business representatives, not children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through our business enquiry forms. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted information so we can review and address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers or applicable legal requirements change. The revised version and update date will be posted on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain fresh consent.
