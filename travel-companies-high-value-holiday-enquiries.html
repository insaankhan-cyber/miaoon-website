How Can Travel Companies Generate Enquiries for High-Value Holiday Packages?

Premium holidays are decided slowly and carefully — here is how travel companies can earn trust and turn interest into genuine enquiries.

MT

Miaoon Strategy Team

Nov 3, 2026 · 8 min read

Summary
  • High-value holiday packages require a marketing approach focused on trust and customer intent.
  • Travel companies should create destination-specific pages that explain the complete experience.
  • SEO, paid advertising, video content and email marketing can attract potential travellers.
  • Strong enquiry forms and timely follow-ups help turn travel interest into bookings.

Selling a high-value holiday package is different from selling a low-cost travel deal. Customers planning luxury holidays, international tours, family vacations or customised travel experiences usually take more time before making a decision. They compare destinations, hotels, itineraries, inclusions, travel dates and overall costs before contacting a travel company.

For travel businesses, the challenge is not simply getting more website visitors. It is attracting people who are genuinely interested in a particular travel experience and are willing to spend on it. Miaoon.in helps businesses build digital marketing strategies that improve online visibility, attract relevant audiences and generate meaningful enquiries.

1. Target Travellers Based on Their Travel Intent

Not every person searching for a destination is ready to purchase a holiday package. Some may be researching places to visit, while others may already be comparing tour operators and package prices.

Travel companies should understand these different stages of customer intent.

For example:

  • Informational: Best places to visit in Switzerland
  • Planning: Switzerland itinerary for 7 days
  • Comparison: Switzerland holiday packages with hotels
  • Booking intent: Luxury Switzerland tour package with flights

A marketing strategy should cover these stages instead of focusing only on broad destination keywords.

Use Google Keyword Planner and Google Trends to explore destination-related searches and seasonal interest. Google's SEO Starter Guide can also help businesses improve their website content.

2. Create Dedicated Landing Pages for High-Value Packages

A single page listing every holiday package may not provide enough information to convince customers to enquire. High-value packages need dedicated landing pages that explain the experience in detail.

A good package page should include:

  • Destination and travel duration
  • Day-wise itinerary
  • Hotel categories and accommodation details
  • Inclusions and exclusions
  • Transport and sightseeing information
  • Customisation options
  • Starting price or quotation details
  • Clear enquiry and consultation options

Use genuine destination photographs and videos wherever possible. Make sure images represent the actual destination or experience and do not create misleading expectations.

Travel companies can use Google Business Profile to maintain their business information and Google Analytics to understand visitor behaviour.

3. Use SEO to Attract Travellers Before They Book

Travel planning often starts weeks or months before the actual trip. This gives travel companies an opportunity to reach potential customers through useful search content.

Instead of publishing only package announcements, create destination guides, itinerary suggestions, travel checklists and comparison articles.

For example, a company selling luxury European holidays could publish content around:

  • Best European destinations for couples
  • Luxury hotels in Switzerland
  • Europe itinerary for families
  • Best time to visit Italy
  • Switzerland versus Austria for a holiday

These articles can link naturally to relevant package pages. This allows readers to move from research to enquiry when they are ready.

Follow Google's Search Essentials, helpful content guidance and structured data documentation when improving your website.

4. Use Paid Advertising to Reach High-Intent Customers

Paid advertising can help travel companies reach people searching for specific holiday experiences. However, campaigns should be designed around the value and type of package being promoted.

For example, a luxury Maldives package should not use the same messaging as a budget group tour. The audience, creative, landing page and enquiry process should reflect the actual offering.

Consider separate campaigns for:

  • Luxury international holidays
  • Honeymoon packages
  • Family vacations
  • Premium group tours
  • Customised international itineraries
  • Seasonal holiday packages

Google Ads can help reach people actively searching for travel options. Social media advertising can introduce destinations and experiences to relevant audiences.

Explore Google Ads, Google Ads conversion tracking, Meta Business, Meta lead ads and Instagram for Business.

5. Use Video Content to Present the Travel Experience

High-value holidays are often purchased because customers want a particular experience, not just a hotel booking or flight ticket.

Short videos can help communicate the feeling of a destination. Travel companies can create content featuring hotel rooms, scenic views, local experiences, sightseeing activities and sample itineraries.

Useful video formats include:

  • Destination walkthroughs
  • Hotel and resort tours
  • Day-wise itinerary videos
  • Customer travel experiences
  • Travel tips and planning guides
  • Short destination comparisons

Publish these videos on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Make sure the content is accurate and clearly identifies what is included in the package.

6. Create Enquiry Forms That Help Qualify Travellers

High-value travel enquiries need more context than a simple name and phone number. A short qualification form can help the sales team understand what the customer is planning.

Consider collecting:

  • Preferred destination
  • Expected travel dates
  • Number of travellers
  • Budget range
  • Type of holiday
  • Departure city
  • Customisation requirements

Keep the form simple and avoid asking for unnecessary personal details. Once the enquiry is submitted, provide a clear confirmation and explain when the travel team will respond.

7. Follow Up with Personalised Travel Recommendations

A customer enquiring about a premium holiday may need help comparing hotels, routes, activities and travel dates. A generic message with a brochure may not be enough.

The sales team should understand the customer's requirements and share suitable options. Follow-ups can include itinerary suggestions, hotel comparisons, answers to common questions and details about package inclusions.

A CRM helps travel companies organise enquiries and maintain communication throughout the decision-making process. Businesses can explore Zoho CRM, HubSpot CRM, Salesforce and Freshsales.

Email platforms such as Mailchimp and Brevo can also support relevant customer communication with appropriate consent.

8. Measure Enquiry Quality and Booking Performance

A campaign that generates many enquiries may still perform poorly if very few customers book a holiday. Travel companies should measure the entire customer journey.

Important metrics include:

  • Cost per qualified enquiry
  • Enquiry-to-consultation rate
  • Consultation-to-booking rate
  • Average booking value
  • Revenue by destination or campaign
  • Repeat bookings and referrals

Use Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio and PageSpeed Insights to monitor different aspects of website and campaign performance.

FAQs

How can travel companies attract customers for luxury holiday packages?
They can use destination-specific SEO, premium package landing pages, targeted advertising, video content and personalised travel consultations to attract suitable customers.

Which digital marketing channel is best for travel companies?
There is no single best channel for every business. Search advertising can reach people actively planning a trip, while SEO, social media and video content can build awareness and support customer research.

What should a high-value holiday package landing page include?
It should explain the itinerary, accommodation, inclusions, exclusions, travel duration, pricing information and customisation options, along with a clear enquiry form.

How can travel companies improve the quality of enquiries?
Ask relevant questions about the destination, travel dates, number of travellers, budget and holiday preferences. This helps the sales team understand whether the enquiry matches the packages offered.

Conclusion

Generating enquiries for high-value holiday packages requires more than attractive travel photographs and discounted offers. Travel companies need to reach the right audience, provide detailed package information and build confidence throughout the customer's decision-making journey.
