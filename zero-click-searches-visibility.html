Zero-Click Searches: How Businesses Can Build Visibility When Users Don’t Click on Websites

Customers are finding answers without clicking — here is how to stay visible and still win enquiries.

MT

Miaoon Strategy Team

Nov 3, 2026 · 7 min read

Summary
  • Zero-click searches are changing how customers discover and evaluate businesses online.
  • Search visibility now goes beyond website clicks, including AI answers, featured snippets and local listings.
  • Businesses need a connected strategy involving SEO, content, brand visibility and digital marketing.
  • Miaoon helps brands improve their online presence through strategic content, SEO and performance-focused marketing.

Search engines are changing the way people find information. Today, a potential customer can search for a service, compare businesses, read reviews and find contact details without visiting multiple websites. This behaviour is known as a zero-click search.

For businesses, this creates an important question: if customers are finding information without clicking on websites, how can a brand remain visible and attract enquiries?

At Miaoon, we look at this as a broader digital marketing challenge. Businesses need more than website traffic. They need a strategy that helps customers discover their brand, understand its value and take action across different digital platforms.

1. Why Zero-Click Searches Matter for Business Growth

A zero-click search happens when users find the information they need directly on a search results page. This can happen through featured snippets, knowledge panels, local listings, instant answers and AI-generated summaries.

For example, someone searching for a digital marketing agency in Delhi may see business listings, reviews, contact details and website information before visiting any website.

Pew Research Center's analysis of US browsing behaviour in March 2025 found that users clicked a traditional search result in 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared, compared with 15% when it did not. This is a study-specific finding, not a universal rate for every search or market.

Source: Pew Research Center.

For businesses, the focus should shift from simply generating clicks to building visibility and trust wherever customers search.

2. How Miaoon Helps Businesses Build Search Visibility

A strong digital presence requires multiple marketing activities to work together. Miaoon approaches this through content, SEO, brand strategy and performance marketing.

2.1 SEO That Builds Long-Term Visibility

Search engine optimisation helps businesses make their websites easier to discover when potential customers search for relevant products or services.

Miaoon's SEO and content approach can help businesses work on:

  • Keyword research based on customer search intent.
  • Website structure and internal linking.
  • Technical SEO and indexability.
  • Useful service pages and blog content.
  • On-page optimisation and content improvements.

For example, a healthcare business may want to reach people searching for specific treatments or diagnostic services. A focused SEO strategy can help organise relevant service information and improve its visibility for appropriate searches.

Google explains its foundational SEO practices in its SEO Starter Guide, crawling and indexing documentation, structured data guide and helpful content guidance.

2.2 Content Strategy That Answers Customer Questions

Customers often search for information before deciding which business to contact. If a website only talks about its services without addressing customer concerns, it may miss valuable opportunities.

Miaoon can help businesses develop content around:

  • Frequently asked customer questions.
  • Product and service comparisons.
  • Industry-specific problems.
  • Buying considerations.
  • Educational blogs and detailed service pages.

This makes the website more useful to visitors while giving search engines clearer information about the business.

Explore Google's content guidelines, title link documentation and snippet documentation.

2.3 GEO and AEO for Changing Search Experiences

Customers are increasingly using AI-powered search experiences to explore topics and compare options. This makes it important for businesses to publish accurate, structured and genuinely useful information.

Miaoon can bring SEO, Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) considerations into a connected content strategy.

This includes clear explanations, descriptive headings, relevant supporting information and consistent business details. However, no agency can guarantee that a search engine or AI platform will feature or cite a particular website.

For further guidance, explore Google's AI features documentation, Google's generative AI content guidance, OpenAI's ChatGPT Search information and Bing Webmaster Guidelines.

3. How Miaoon Connects Search Visibility with Customer Enquiries

Visibility becomes more valuable when it supports actual business goals. A website may receive impressions and traffic but still generate very few qualified enquiries.

This is where different digital marketing activities need to work together.

Miaoon can help businesses connect organic content with paid campaigns, landing pages and conversion measurement. For instance, a real estate business may use SEO to build long-term visibility for property-related searches while paid advertising supports the promotion of a specific project.

The landing page, enquiry form and follow-up journey should all communicate the same offer clearly.

Businesses can understand their performance using Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio and Google Ads.

Miaoon's analytics and optimisation services can be relevant when businesses need to understand what is working and where their digital experience needs improvement.

4. What Businesses Should Measure Beyond Website Traffic

Zero-click searches make it important to look at a wider set of performance indicators.

Businesses should review:

  • Search impressions and branded search activity.
  • Visibility for important service-related keywords.
  • Local listing interactions.
  • Website enquiries and qualified leads.
  • Phone calls and appointment requests.
  • Conversion rates and revenue contribution.

Google Search Console provides search performance information, while Google Analytics helps businesses understand activity on their websites. These tools should be interpreted together rather than treating any single metric as proof of business growth.

Useful resources include Google Analytics help, Google Business Profile, Google's local ranking guidance and PageSpeed Insights.

5. Why Businesses Need a Connected Digital Marketing Strategy

A business cannot depend on one source of visibility forever. Search behaviour, customer expectations and advertising platforms continue to change.

Miaoon helps businesses approach these activities as connected parts of digital growth. Through its content strategy services, paid advertising services and broader digital marketing services, the focus is on creating a consistent brand presence and making marketing activity more purposeful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are zero-click searches bad for businesses?
Not necessarily. They may reduce website clicks for certain queries, but they can still create brand exposure and help users discover business information directly on search platforms.

Can SEO still help businesses with zero-click searches?
Yes. SEO helps businesses organise their website content, improve discoverability and build visibility for relevant searches, including searches where users may eventually visit a website or contact the business.

How can Miaoon help businesses adapt to zero-click searches?
Miaoon can help businesses improve their SEO, content strategy, digital advertising and performance measurement so their brand remains visible across relevant online discovery channels.

What is the role of GEO and AEO in zero-click searches?
GEO and AEO focus on making information easier to understand and discover in AI-powered and answer-based search experiences. They complement broader SEO practices.
